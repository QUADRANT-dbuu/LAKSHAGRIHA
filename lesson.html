Newton's First Law: Inertia

Newton’s First Law states that an object at rest stays at rest, and an object in motion stays in motion with the same speed and in the same direction unless acted upon by an unbalanced force. This concept is known as Inertia.

Inertia is directly proportional to Mass. A heavy object has more inertia and is harder to move or stop.
