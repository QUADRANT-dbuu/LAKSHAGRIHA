Newton's 2nd Law: The Math

F = m × a

Newton found that Acceleration happens when a Force acts on a Mass. The more mass an object has, the more force you need to accelerate it!

Key Idea: If you double the Force, you double the Acceleration. But if you double the Mass, the Acceleration drops by half!
